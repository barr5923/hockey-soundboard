Game Rules

SKAHA / Sno-King Amateur Hockey Association - 12U

Quick reference for 12U C recreational / Metropolitan Hockey League (MHL) games. Select, tournament, and playoff formats may differ. Confirm your game's rules with the officials and team manager. This summary is not a replacement for the official rules.

Sources checked October 5, 2026. USA Hockey playing-rule cycle: 2025-2029. Local pages do not identify a season-specific edition.

Game clock - Sno-King 12U C

  • Ice slot: 60 minutes, with a 5-minute warm-up.
  • Periods 1 and 2: 15 minutes each, running clock.
  • Period 3: 12 minutes, stop clock, subject to ice curfew.
  • Timeouts: MHL does not allow timeouts during regular-season games.

Sources: Sno-King game-day resources and MHL game clock.

Minor penalties - MHL 12U

  • Running clock: 3:00 minutes.
  • Stop clock: 1:00 minute.
  • MHL instructs scorekeepers to halve penalty time remaining at the end of period 2 when transitioning to stop-clock play. Confirm the adjustment with the officials.
  • These are MHL minor-penalty timings, not a universal 12U penalty duration. For majors, misconducts, or other penalties, follow the officials and applicable rulebook.

Source: MHL minor-penalty and game-clock rules.

Ice curfew

  • Home scorekeepers and officials must finish within the scheduled ice slot; an overrun requires Sno-King Hockey and arena staff approval.
  • Before period 3, tell the referees how much actual ice time remains.
  • If fewer than 24 minutes remain for a normal 12-minute stop-clock period, Sno-King requires shortening it to half the remaining ice time.
  • Example: 20 minutes left in the ice slot means a 10-minute stop-clock third period. Coordinate with referees; they inform the coaches.

Source: Sno-King ice-curfew guidance.

USA Hockey 12U playing rules

  • No body checking in 12U games. Legal body contact is different from body checking.
  • Immediate offside: 12U does not use the delayed/tag-up offside exception for older divisions.
  • Icing while shorthanded: 12U does not receive the older-division exemption; normal icing rules still apply on a penalty kill.
  • Neck-laceration protection: required for youth players under the current USA Hockey rules.

Sources: USA Hockey's body-contact / checking explanation and 2025-2029 rule updates.

Official references and event exceptions

Sno-King directs playing-rule questions to USA Hockey and PNAHA. Its game-day page currently links older rulebook editions; use the current USA Hockey rulebook for the 2025-2029 cycle. Check the event's published rules for overtime, shootouts, mercy rules, and tournament-specific timing rather than assuming a standard.
